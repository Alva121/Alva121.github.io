Privacy Policy

Effective: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GeoPhoto handles information when you use the app. GeoPhoto is a field-survey GPS photography application that can capture photographs, attach location information, organize photos into projects, display stored locations on maps, and export project data.

GeoPhoto does not require an account and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or an advertising-ID integration.

1. Information GeoPhoto handles

  • Precise or approximate device location, including latitude, longitude, accuracy, provider and fix age.
  • Photographs captured with the app.
  • Project names, filenames, capture identifiers, timestamps and survey metadata.
  • Exported project data such as XLSX and KML files.
  • Technical information required by mapping and platform services.

2. Storage

Captured photographs and related sidecar metadata are stored in user-accessible storage selected through Android's storage framework. App-private databases and preferences are used to maintain indexes and operational state.

GeoPhoto does not contain an app-defined server that automatically uploads your photographs or project files. You may explicitly share files or save them to a storage provider that you choose.

Depending on Android device and backup settings, eligible app-private data may also be included in operating-system-managed backup or device-transfer services.

3. Location

Location is used for survey coordinates, GPS accuracy, photo stamps, map placement and project exports. Location information may be written into the photo, EXIF metadata, GeoPhoto sidecar metadata, local indexes and export files.

GeoPhoto does not request Android background-location permission.

4. Camera and media access

Camera permission is used to capture survey photographs. GeoPhoto may request Android media-location access when required to read original location metadata from saved images for verification.

5. Google Maps and Google Play services

GeoPhoto uses Google Maps SDK for Android for interactive map features and Google Play services for location functionality. Google may receive technical and usage information according to its own policies.

GeoPhoto does not use Google Maps imagery as the permanent map image burned into survey photographs.

Google Privacy Policy

6. OpenStreetMap

When an online mini-map is generated for a photo stamp, GeoPhoto may request a small number of tiles from OpenStreetMap's public tile service. Requests reveal the requested map area and normal network information such as IP address and the app User-Agent.

GeoPhoto does not use the OpenStreetMap public tile service to download offline map regions. When the map cannot be loaded, GeoPhoto uses a locally rendered offline location panel.

7. Android Geocoder

GeoPhoto may use Android's Geocoder service to resolve location information for display. The provider and network implementation can vary by Android device.

8. Sharing and exports

GeoPhoto can share selected photos and create XLSX/KML exports. Sharing occurs only after you select a destination using Android's sharing or storage interfaces. Receiving apps/providers may apply their own privacy policies.

9. Analytics, advertising and payments

GeoPhoto does not include advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, Crashlytics, an advertising-ID integration, subscriptions, in-app purchases or payment processing.

10. Retention and deletion

Survey photographs, sidecars and exports remain in the storage location you selected until deleted. Clearing app data removes app-private state but does not necessarily remove photographs or exports stored in shared/user-selected storage.

11. Security

GeoPhoto uses Android platform security controls, scoped storage and user-granted permissions. No storage or transmission mechanism can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Children's privacy

GeoPhoto is a field-survey utility and is not designed specifically for children.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated when GeoPhoto features, services or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated accordingly.
