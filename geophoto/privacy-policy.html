GeoPhoto

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GeoPhoto handles information when you use the app. GeoPhoto is a field-survey GPS photography application that can capture photographs, attach location information, organize photos into projects, display stored locations on maps, and export project data.

GeoPhoto does not require an account and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or an advertising-ID integration.

1. Information GeoPhoto handles

  • Precise or approximate device location, including latitude, longitude, accuracy, provider and fix age.
  • Photographs captured with the app.
  • Project names, filenames, capture identifiers, timestamps and survey metadata.
  • Exported project data such as XLSX and KML files.
  • Technical information required by mapping and platform services.

2. Storage and backup

Captured photographs and related GeoPhoto sidecar metadata are stored in user-accessible storage selected through Android's storage framework.

GeoPhoto also uses app-private databases, preferences and temporary files for operational purposes such as capture processing, recovery and rebuilding the photo library.

GeoPhoto does not contain an app-defined server that automatically uploads your photographs or project files. You may explicitly share files or save them to a storage provider that you choose.

GeoPhoto disables Android cloud backup of its app-private data and excludes that data from Android device-to-device transfer. This includes operational capture jobs, temporary capture files, the rebuildable photo index and app preferences.

After reinstalling GeoPhoto or moving to another device, you may need to reconnect your existing GeoPhoto storage folder so the app can rebuild the published photo library.

Photos, GeoPhoto sidecars, project metadata and exports stored in user-selected shared storage are not part of GeoPhoto's private-app backup. They may still be backed up or transferred independently by your device, storage provider, cloud service or other applications that you choose to use.

3. Location

Location is used for survey coordinates, GPS accuracy, photo stamps, map placement and project exports. Location information may be written into the photo, EXIF metadata, GeoPhoto sidecar metadata, local indexes and export files.

GeoPhoto does not request Android background-location permission.

Live device location is requested only while features that require it are actively being used, such as the Home GPS telemetry or Camera screen. GeoPhoto does not provide a remote live-location tracking service.

4. Camera and media access

Camera permission is used to capture survey photographs.

GeoPhoto may request Android media-location access when required to read original location metadata from saved images for verification.

5. Google Maps and Google Play services

GeoPhoto uses Google Maps SDK for Android for interactive map features and Google Play services for location functionality.

Google may receive technical and usage information according to its own policies when these services are used.

GeoPhoto does not use Google Maps imagery as the permanent map image burned into survey photographs.

6. OpenStreetMap

When an online mini-map is generated for a photo stamp, GeoPhoto may request a small number of tiles from OpenStreetMap's public tile service.

These requests reveal the requested map area and normal network information such as the device's IP address and the GeoPhoto application User-Agent.

GeoPhoto does not use the OpenStreetMap public tile service to download offline map regions.

When an online map cannot be loaded, GeoPhoto uses a locally rendered offline location panel instead.

Online map stamps display OpenStreetMap attribution.

OpenStreetMap copyright and attribution

7. Android Geocoder

GeoPhoto may use Android's Geocoder service to resolve location information for display.

The geocoding provider and network implementation can vary by Android device. Captured coordinates may therefore be processed by the geocoding service configured by the device.

8. Sharing and exports

GeoPhoto can share selected photographs and create project exports such as XLSX and KML files.

Sharing occurs only after you select a destination using Android's sharing or storage interfaces.

Files shared with another application, cloud service or storage provider may contain survey coordinates and other project metadata. Those services may process the files according to their own privacy policies.

9. Analytics, advertising and payments

GeoPhoto does not include advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, Crashlytics, an advertising-ID integration, subscriptions, in-app purchases or payment processing.

Third-party platform components such as Google Maps and Google Play services may perform their own service-related diagnostics and telemetry according to their respective policies.

10. Retention and deletion

Survey photographs, GeoPhoto sidecars, project metadata and exports remain in the storage location you selected until they are deleted.

GeoPhoto also maintains app-private operational data while the application is installed. This may include temporary capture-processing information and rebuildable indexes.

Clearing GeoPhoto's application data removes its app-private state but does not necessarily remove photographs, sidecars, project metadata or exports stored in user-selected shared storage.

If the published GeoPhoto storage remains available, the app can reconnect to that storage and rebuild its photo library from the surviving published records.

11. Security

GeoPhoto uses Android platform security controls, application sandboxing, scoped storage and user-granted permissions.

GeoPhoto does not include a GeoPhoto-controlled remote live-location tracking server or background location tracking service.

No storage or transmission mechanism can be guaranteed completely secure. You should protect your device and any survey records that you export or share.

12. Children's privacy

GeoPhoto is a field-survey utility and is not designed specifically for children.

GeoPhoto does not provide account-based data collection directed specifically at children.

13. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when GeoPhoto features, third-party services, security practices or legal requirements change.

The effective date displayed at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.
